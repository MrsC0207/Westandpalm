What we created

A responsive multi-page website with a strong visual identity, structured service journeys, SEO foundations, social-sharing metadata and an architecture designed to expand with the business.

LWN Social

A member-run community needed more than a brochure site. It needed one useful place for residents to discover what was happening and get involved.

The brief

Bring dining, activities, special events and community information together in a friendly, easy-to-use site that works just as well on a phone as it does at home.

What we created

A responsive community platform with event and activity structures, category pages, subscriber communications, social-sharing support and custom functionality designed around how the group actually operates.
