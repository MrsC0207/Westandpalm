Good to know

Pricing without surprises.

Are these fixed packages?

No. They are useful starting points. Your proposal reflects the pages, content and functionality your project actually needs.

What about third-party costs?

Domains, hosting plans, licensed services, IDX, booking platforms and other third-party subscriptions are separate unless your proposal specifically says otherwise.

How are larger projects priced?

More complex websites are scoped individually. Larger builds may use staged payments tied to agreed project milestones.

What if I need something later?

You can use a care plan, request ad-hoc support or ask for a separate quote for larger additions.
