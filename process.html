How it works

Six stages. No mystery.

You always know what happens next.

01

Discover

We start with a conversation about your business, your audience, what you want the website to achieve and what isn’t working today. You don’t need a technical brief. We’ll ask the questions that help uncover what the project actually needs.

02

Define

We turn that conversation into a clear plan: pages, content, functionality, integrations, timing and scope. You’ll receive a project proposal and price before work begins, so there are no surprises halfway through.

03

Design

We establish a visual direction that feels right for your business, then shape the page experience around your content and customers. This is where typography, imagery, hierarchy and personality begin working together.

04

Build

The approved direction becomes a responsive website, built to work across phones, tablets and larger screens. We add the agreed functionality, integrations and SEO foundations while keeping performance and usability in mind.

05

Refine

You review the site as a whole and we work through the agreed revisions. We test links, forms, layouts and key screen sizes, then polish the details that make the finished site feel considered rather than merely complete.

06

Launch

Once everything is approved, we handle the final technical pieces: domain connection, SSL, indexing, sitemap, Search Console and launch checks as appropriate. Then your new website is ready to meet the world.

No mystery. No jargon.

Many standard website projects can be completed in around two to four weeks once the content and required access are available. Larger sites, complex integrations and projects where content is still being developed may take longer, and we’ll set expectations before we begin.
